### Section G: Clinical & Sports Case Studies
71. **Correction:** Plant cells have rigid cell walls that make stems stiff and stationary. Human cells lack cell walls, which allows our tissues and joints to remain flexible and agile for athletic movement.
72. **Answer:** Vacuoles store cellular water. When severe dehydration occurs, blood volume decreases, causing blood pressure to drop and forcing the heart to beat dangerously fast to circulate oxygen.
73. **Correction:** Grammar error: *"I never wear a life jacket"* (routine habit). Safety risk: Even strong swimmers can be knocked unconscious in river rapids. A life jacket keeps the head above water, protecting the respiratory system from drowning.
74. **Answer:** It is a plant cell. The presence of a rigid cell wall, chloroplasts (giving a green hue), and a large central vacuole confirms it is plant tissue.
75. **Answer:** The mitochondria. They require glucose (from food) and oxygen (from respiration) to synthesize ATP energy molecules.
76. **Correction:** *"The heart **is** an organ that **pumps** blood to all tissues."*
77. **Answer:** The climbing helmet. The cranium (skull bones) encloses and protects the delicate brain tissue.
78. **Answer:** Unicellular organisms consist of a single cell (e.g., bacteria or yeast). Multicellular organisms consist of many coordinated cells (e.g., humans or trees). Humans are multicellular.
79. **Correction:** Cells form tissues; tissues form organs; organs form organ systems; and organ systems form the complete organism.
80. **Answer:** Water conducts heat away from the body 25 times faster than air. A neoprene wetsuit traps a thin layer of water warmed by body heat, preventing hypothermia and regulating body temperature.
